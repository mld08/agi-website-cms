Before you write

Quick answers.

How long until I get a response?

Two business days for most enquiries. Press requests on a developing story are usually answered the same day, please mark them URGENT in the subject.

Do you accept research collaborations from outside Africa?

Yes, provided the work is led with, not for, African researchers and institutions. Send a one-page note describing the question, the team, and the timeline.

Can I cite your reports?

All AGI publications are free to read and cite under the citation guidelines printed on the inside cover of each report. Long-form reproduction requires written permission.

Are you hiring?

Open positions, fellowships and internships are announced on our LinkedIn page. We always welcome unsolicited expressions of interest at contact@africagovernanceinstitute.org.

How can I support your work?

The cleanest way is institutional partnership, joint research, programme co-funding, or fiscal sponsorship of a fellowship. Write to our partnerships team at contact@africagovernanceinstitute.org.
